Quick setup

Four steps, about two minutes per account.

  1. Allow incognito access (one time)

    This lets the extension capture a second account from an incognito window without logging out of your main one.

    Open chrome://extensions → Claude Usage Monitor → Details → turn on Allow in Incognito.

  2. Capture your main account

    Sign in to claude.ai in this browser, then click Detect signed-in sessions on the Accounts page and press Add.

  3. Capture each extra account

    Open an incognito window (Ctrl+Shift+N), sign in to the next account, come back here and click Detect → Add.

    ⚠ Then close the incognito window — do not click “Log out” in Claude. Logging out kills the session the extension is using. Close all incognito windows before signing in to the next account (they share one cookie jar).

    Account in another browser or Chrome profile? Open claude.ai there → DevTools (F12) → Application → Cookies → https://claude.ai → copy the sessionKey value and paste it in Add by session key.

  4. Pick your alert levels

    Defaults: warning at 75%, critical at 90%, checks every 5 minutes. Change them under Alerts & thresholds, then hit Send test alert to make sure Windows/macOS notifications are allowed for Chrome.
